Privacy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Deepwork is a personal workspace for planning and collaborating on work. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

Deepwork is local-first. Your workspace is kept in your browser unless you choose to use cloud sync or collaboration features.

Information you provide

Your workspace can contain boards, cards, comments, messages, whiteboards, profile details, and collaboration activity. If you sign in with Google, Deepwork receives basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture). If you enable Google Calendar features, it requests access to create or update calendar events.

Local storage and encryption

Workspace information and preferences are stored in your browser. Cloud backup data is encrypted in your browser before upload. The encryption key is kept in your browser or provided through a secure invite, so losing the key may make an encrypted backup unrecoverable.

Some information needed to make collaboration work—such as account email, display name, board membership, invite details, and notification routing—is processed by the service. Do not treat every field or every feature as end-to-end encrypted. People you invite to a shared board can see the content available to them in that board.

Service providers

When you use online features, information may pass through Google (sign-in and optional calendar integration), Vercel (website and API hosting), Render (call signaling), and MongoDB Atlas (cloud data storage). These providers process information to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy notices.

Calls and permissions

Audio and video calls use your device’s camera and microphone only when you grant browser permission and start or join a call. WebRTC carries media between call participants when network conditions allow; signaling is used to establish the connection. Screen sharing starts only when you choose it.

Usage information

The app may keep basic activity events on your device to power its own analytics and progress views. Hosting providers may collect routine technical logs, such as request and device information, to operate and protect their services. Deepwork does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can use guest mode, decline optional browser permissions, disconnect Google integration, export your workspace, or clear local browser data. Clearing browser data may also remove your local encryption key. You can stop using cloud sync and collaboration features at any time.

Children and changes

Deepwork is a general productivity tool and is not designed for children under 13. This policy may change as the app changes; the latest version will be posted on this page with its update date.

Privacy questions

For a privacy question or request, contact the person who provided or operates your Deepwork deployment.
